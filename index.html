The palette

How it works: each pigment is modeled as a reflectance spectrum; mixing takes the weighted geometric mean of spectra (the standard approximation for real paint), converted through CIE color math. It is a toy model, not paint chemistry — real pigments vary by brand, and ratios are starting points. Mix a test batch and judge by eye.
